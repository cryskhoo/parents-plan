How we keep this fair

  • Same start date and the same S$10,000 for every line. Everything converted to Singapore dollars at that day's rate.
  • Fund prices are already after the fund manager's yearly fee, on both sides. We don't double count it.
  • Platform charges, bonuses, broker commissions, FX and custody fees are added in the cost and projection sections, with the source named.
  • ETF dividends are reinvested (we use accumulating ETFs or adjusted prices). AIA fund prices include reinvested income.
  • Things that don't fit in a chart: free fund switches, a human adviser, and the discipline of a plan are on the AIA side; flexibility to sell any day is on the ETF side.
